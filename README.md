## ✨ Hii, I'm Vinnie 💅✨
I'm an 🚫Anti-GenAI🚫 Game Developer graduated as Technician in Digital Games at PUC-Campinas, and currently working as IT Support Analyst at Manacás - PUCC

I love coding and programming, so all code of mine was, is and will continue to be written by my own two hands, character by character, line by line, even the most mundane and repetitive for loops, cuz no AI, not even autocomplete, will take away the joy I feel writing my own stuff!

I also love Videogames, wich is the reason I studied 5 years of Computer Science at UNESP Rio Claro before changing my graduation to specifically design and develop games

That said, you can find all my game projects both here or at [my itch.io portfolio page](https://vprrkmhsy.itch.io/), where you can actually play most of them (wich would make me happy :3)

I also have other projects here, some not necessarily game related, that you can check out if it interests you
